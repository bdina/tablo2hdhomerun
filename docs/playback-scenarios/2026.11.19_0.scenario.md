### 6.1 Observation 1 — `CBS 4.1` ~07:15 EST Segment Replay, Decoder Flood, and 167s Stall Blindness

Three distinct mechanisms combined around `07:14:48 – 07:20:03 EST` to produce the visible replay/oscillation and delayed stall recovery instead of a clean stationary video freeze:

#### Mechanism A — Post-Freeze PTS Desync, `ptsDeltaTicks` Rebase & Unfiltered Input Regression ([`MpegTsSync.scala:711-746`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L711-L746), [`1120-1132`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L1120-L1132))
1. **Unconditional `ptsDeficitMs` Re-Triggering**: In `MpegTsSync.normalizePacketTimestamps` (`L1120-L1131`), `mediaClockAnchorPts` is anchored once at stream startup and only nudged forward if `expectedPts < lastOutputPts`. Whenever live segments slip permanently `>= 10s` behind real-time wall clock (`seq=281` `15.46s` and `seq=297` `12.01s`), `ptsDeficitTicks >= 900,000` (`10,000 ms`) stays latched *permanently* even after live video resumes (`L1230-L1237`). Between `07:11:23` and `07:14:48 EST`, `FreezeFramePacer` therefore fired **19 times** on normal `164–2,948 ms` inter-segment gaps because `ptsDeficitMs` hovered at `10,003–11,101 ms`.
2. **Backward PTS Emission Upon Resume (`L733-L737`)**: During each `1..13` burst activation, `lastOutputPts` advances monotonically by `3,003` ticks/frame (`33.37 ms`). When a live keyframe resumes, if `lastOutputPts - (inPts + ptsDeltaTicks)` is `< RebaseThresholdTicks` (`90,000` ticks = `1.0s`, as occurred in 17 of those bursts), execution falls into the `else` branch at [`MpegTsSync.scala:733-L737`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L733-L737), leaves `ptsDeltaTicks` unadjusted, and emits **`outPts = mappedPts < lastOutputPts`** — writing **out-of-order / past presentation timestamps** into Plex's FFmpeg demuxer.
3. **Overlap / Re-Encoded Frame Acceptance After `RebaseThresholdTicks`**: Once a freeze exceeds `~30` synthetic frames (`> 1.0s`), `diffFromOut < -90,000` triggers [`MpegTsSync.scala:728-732`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L728-L732) to rebase `ptsDeltaTicks = lastOutputPts + 3003 - inPts`. Because [`MpegTsSync.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala) never tracks a monotonic high-water mark for **raw source input PTS** (`maxSeenInputVideoPts`), any older or re-encoded buffered frames flushed by Tablo after a tuner hiccup (or across mega-segments `seq=281`/`297`) are accepted with newly shifted forward output PTS and played again as "new" video (~`07:14:48` / `~07:15 EST` replay).

#### Mechanism B — Malformed MPEG-2 Synthetic P-Frames in [`FreezeFramePacer.buildMpeg2FreezePayload`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/FreezeFramePacer.scala#L690-L716)
Inspecting [`FreezeFramePacer.scala:695-714`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/FreezeFramePacer.scala#L695-L714):

1. **Wrong Extension ID (`0x11` instead of `0x8F`)**: Byte `4` has upper nibble `0x1_` (`extension_start_code_identifier = 1`, which in ISO/IEC 13818-2 is **`sequence_extension_id`**), NOT `0x8_` (**`picture_coding_extension_id = 8`**)! FFmpeg's `mpeg2video` decoder immediately rejects it with **`ignoring seq ext after 100`**, leaves `picture_structure` unconfigured for this P-frame, and emits `Warning MVs not available`.
2. **Invalid Truncated Slice VLCs (`00 00 01 <row> 38 00`)**: Each slice row (`L706-L711`) emits a quantizer scale (`0x38`) followed by `0x00`, which is an invalid VLC bitstring rather than valid first-and-last-macroblock skipped P-frame addressing (`macroblock_address_increment = 1` + `MC_Not_Coded` + escape-coded jump to `width/16`). Consequently FFmpeg throws **60 macroblock/texture/MV errors per synthetic frame** (`Invalid mb type in P-frame at 3 23`, `00 motion_type at 2 15`, `ac-tex damaged`), corrupting/cycling its internal reference frames instead of cleanly repeating the last frame. On `CBS 4.1` (`media-01050.ts`..`media-01305.ts`), Stage-2 VAAPI kept encoding those oscillating reference blocks at `20.5 Mbps` (`2.56 MB/s`) and spinning PMS's `10.48 MB` log files every `33.6s`.

#### Mechanism C — Off-By-One `isReset` Masking Stalls Below 3,600 Segments ([`HlsPlaylistPoller.scala:153-159`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsPlaylistPoller.scala#L153-L159))

### 1. Prevent `CBS 4.1` Segment Replay & Freeze Cleanly on Signal Loss
- **Action 1A — Enforce Monotonic Input PTS High-Water Mark & Clean Post-Freeze Sync in [`MpegTsSync.scala:711-746`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L711-L746) & [`1120-1132`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L1120-L1132)**:
