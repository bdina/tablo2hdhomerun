## 4. Root Cause Summary & Resolution

1. **Defect 1 — Freeze-Frame Silent ADTS AAC Audio Emitted at `0.64×` Real-Time (`-360 ms/sec` Drift)** → Resolved in [`f415a15`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun):
   - `MpegTsSync.CacheStage.onTimer` previously emitted 1 silent audio frame per `3003`-tick (`33.37 ms`) video frame regardless of codec duration (`21.33 ms` for `48 kHz` ADTS AAC = `0.640s/sec`, `32.00 ms` for AC-3 = `0.959s/sec`).
   - Added `FreezeFramePacer.audioFrameStepTicks` (exact ADTS `sampling_frequency_index` duration lookup: `48 kHz → 1920` ticks, `44.1 kHz → 2090` ticks, `32 kHz → 2880` ticks; AC-3 → `2880` ticks) and a zero-allocation per-PID outbound audio PTS cursor (`emitSilentAudioUpTo`) in `MpegTsSync.CacheStage`, plus monotonic post-synthetic splice guarding (`lastAudioWasSynthetic`) skipping any tiny `<= 2s` overlapping leading live audio packet on resume.
   - Reduces 100-second freeze A/V drift from **`-36,000 ms` to `< 22 ms`**.
2. **Defect 2 — Unbridged Live-Video Audio Gaps (`> 5s`) Latching Permanent Stage-1 `-codec:a copy` Offset** → Resolved in [`b1188e5`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun):
   - Added `CacheStage.maybeFillLiveAudioGaps`: during non-frozen live video (`freezeThreshold > 0`), once a learned audio PID trails `lastOutputPts` by `>= 1.5s` (`135,000` ticks) while actively dropping audio (`isDroppingAudioPid`) or after `>= 30` video frames without audio, `CacheStage` smoothly emits silent codec-matched frames trailing video by a `250 ms` headroom cushion until clean live audio resumes — preventing Plex Stage-1 from ever seeing a `> 5s` audio gap while leaving healthy streams (`< 1.5s`), `AudioLossMonitor`, and `windowAudioFramesClean` metrics completely untouched.
   - Verified across `443/443` tests passing under `-Werror`.
