### New Finding — Benign `"playlist stalled"` Log Spam on Flawless Streams
On the flawless `CBS 4.1` evening stream (`d6138f78`, `18:33:26–19:03+ EST`, `99.7–100.0%` signal in 58/59 windows, `12.97 Mbps` avg, `0` TEI, `0` CC, `0` retunes), the proxy logged **`231` `"playlist stalled, probing session liveness"`** events — of which **`228` (`98.7%`) were false positives** at `stallPolls=4..6` that resumed `~0.6s` later on the very next normal `3.003s` segment (with the lone real `9.38s` RF fade at `18:58:35 EST` reaching `stallPolls=14`). Live inspection of Tablo's `pls.m3u8` revealed two compounding causes:
1. Tablo 4th-Gen violates RFC 8216 by emitting **`#EXT-X-TARGETDURATION:1`** alongside **`#EXTINF:3.003,`** segments; `HlsPlaylistPoller.pollDelayMs` checked `lastSegmentDuration < targetDurationSec` (`3.003 < 1.0 == false`), falling back to `1000ms` instead of `75% × 3.003s = 2252ms` and generating ~3.5 wasted unchanged polls per segment.
2. `sessionProbeIntervalPolls = 4` fired at `~2.62s` (`1.0s + 3 × 0.5s`) — *before* a normal `3.003s` segment is due — emitting false `"playlist stalled"` / `"playlist resumed"` `INFO` pairs and unnecessary `POST /keepalive?lh=1` probes on healthy playback (masked below `seq=3600` until commit `255d354` fixed the sub-3600 stall-counting off-by-one).

## 6. Root Cause Technical Deep Dive

### 6.1 Tablo `#EXT-X-TARGETDURATION:1` Defeating Segment-Aware Polling
In [`HlsPlaylistPoller.pollDelayMs`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsPlaylistPoller.scala#L116-L130) (prior to `3969fe1`), the segment-duration-aware branch required `state.lastSegmentDuration < targetDurationSec.toDouble`. Because Tablo 4th-Gen firmware declares `#EXT-X-TARGETDURATION:1` while publishing `#EXTINF:3.003,` HD segments (and `~2.0s` SD segments), `3.003 < 1.0` evaluated to `false` and fell back to `pollDelaySec`, sleeping only **`1000ms`** after each segment arrival. Poll #1 arrived `~2.0s` early, flipped `lastAdvanced = false`, and dropped the poller to `pollDelayMinMs = 500ms`, causing `34–36` wasted unchanged polls per 30s (`~3.5` per segment).

### 6.2 `sessionProbeIntervalPolls = 4` Firing Inside Normal Segment Intervals
Because `1.0s` (poll 1) + `3 × 0.5s` (polls 2–4) + HTTP RTT totals **`~2.62s`**, [`HlsPlaylistPoller.shouldProbeSession`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsPlaylistPoller.scala#L51-L53) fired at `stallPolls = 4` **before a normal `3.003s` segment was even due**, logging `INFO [stream:hls] playlist stalled, probing session liveness` ([`HlsBackend.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsBackend.scala#L247-L254)) and sending an unnecessary `POST /player/sessions/{token}/keepalive?lh=1` probe — followed `~0.6s` later by `INFO [stream:hls] playlist resumed after stall` ([`HlsBackend.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsBackend.scala#L278-L285)). This behavior was masked below `seq=3600` (`~2–3 hours`) until `255d354` fixed the sub-3600 `isReset` off-by-one.

## 7. Architecture & Design Recommendations

1. **Trust `#EXTINF` Duration Over Bogus `#EXT-X-TARGETDURATION:1` in `pollDelayMs` (Implemented in `3969fe1`)**:
   - **Action**: Updated [`HlsPlaylistPoller.pollDelayMs`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsPlaylistPoller.scala#L116-L130) to use `(state.lastSegmentDuration * 750.0).toLong.max(pollDelayMinMs).min(pollDelayMaxMs)` whenever `playlistAdvanced` is `true` and `state.lastSegmentDuration.isFinite && state.lastSegmentDuration > 0.0`, removing the `< targetDurationSec` guard.
   - **Expected Outcome**: First poll after a `3.003s` CBS segment waits `2252ms` (and `1501ms` after a `2.002s` COZITV segment) instead of `1000ms`, cutting wasted playlist HTTP polls by ~55–65% with zero added playback latency.
2. **Gate Session Liveness Probes & Stall/Resume `INFO` Logs at `sessionProbeMinPolls = 12` (Implemented in `3969fe1`)**:
   - **Action**: Added `sessionProbeMinPolls: Int = 12` in [`HlsPlaylistPoller.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsPlaylistPoller.scala#L9-L10) and gated [`shouldProbeSession`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsPlaylistPoller.scala#L51-L53) on `stallPolls >= sessionProbeMinPolls && (stallPolls - sessionProbeMinPolls) % sessionProbeIntervalPolls == 0` (probing at `12, 16, 20, ...`, i.e., `~6s` of unchanged polls / `~8s` total after a `3s` segment, then every `~2s`), and aligned [`HlsBackend.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsBackend.scala#L278-L285) resume logging on `state.stallPolls >= HlsPlaylistPoller.sessionProbeMinPolls`.
   - **Expected Outcome**: Eliminates 100% of false `"playlist stalled"` / `"playlist resumed"` log spam and redundant `/keepalive` probes during normal `3.0–4.5s` segments and `1s → 3s` commercial splices, while still catching genuine RF stalls (like the `18:58:35` `9.38s` / `stallPolls=14` fade) before `FreezeFramePacer` (`10s`) activates and keeping immediate `404`/`ENDLIST` detection and `60s` `PlaylistStalled` retunes unchanged.
