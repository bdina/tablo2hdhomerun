## 2. Executive Summary of Incident & Resilience Validation

1. **`FreezeFramePacer` Clean Synthetic MPEG-2 Frames (`0f57f35`)**: Across **`43 seconds`** of continuous synthetic MPEG-2 freeze-frame pacing on `CBS 4.1` (`08:11:13 – 08:11:56 EST`), `Plex Media Server.log` recorded **`0` `[mpeg2video]` decode errors** (down from `61` errors per synthetic frame / `293,850` errors on `2026-11-19`). ### Four Lifecycle & Retune Findings Uncovered
1. **Unconditional `IdleGrace` on Unhealthy Disconnect (`08:11:56` & `08:15:03 EST`)**: When Roku disconnected from the frozen/retuning `CBS 4.1` session, `SessionManager.Release` unconditionally moved the dead lease (`3d015de5`) into `180s` `IdleGrace` with an active `Sink.ignore` drain. During the second `IdleGrace` (`08:15:03 – 08:18:03 EST`), the proxy executed **24 background cold retunes (`attempt=9..32`) with zero connected clients**, occupying a tuner slot (`occupied=2/4`) while the user had already moved on to `FOX 25.1`, `NBC 15.1`, and `ABC 9.1`.
2. **Fast-Attach onto a Dead `IdleGrace` Session → 13% Roku Hang (`08:13:28 – 08:15:03 EST`)**: Re-tuning `CBS 4.1` 93 seconds after the first disconnect hit `SessionManager.Acquire` during `IdleGrace` (`grace-cancel`, `fast-attached` in `0ms`). Because the cached session was outputting only synthetic freeze P-frames (`signalQualityPct=0.0`), `AlignToIFrameStage` waited `3000ms` with no live keyframe and forced alignment (`376 dropped packets`), leaving Roku **stuck at 13% loading for `94.2s`** instead of starting a fresh `Opening` tune (which failed cleanly with PMS `Error 16` on `FOX 25.1` and `NBC 15.1`).
3. **Exponential Backoff Resetting to `[1]` (`1s`) on Every Failed `503 no_signal_lock` Retune**: `ResilientHlsSource` configured `RestartSettings(minBackoff = 1s, maxBackoff = 15s, randomFactor = 0.2)` without overriding `maxRestartsWithin`, which defaults to `minBackoff (1s)` in Pekko Streams. Because Tablo took `~6.1s` (`> 1s`) to return `503 no_signal_lock`, Pekko's `"ResetRestartCount"` timer fired during every in-flight attempt — resetting all **31 failed cold retunes (`attempt=2..32`)** back to **`Restarting stream due to failure [1]`** (`1s` delay).
4. **165-Second Wait (`304` Stall Polls) on Stale `200 OK` `pl.m3u8` Before First Cold Retune**: After `CBS 4.1` stalled at `lastSeq=36` (`08:11:12 EST`), Tablo continued answering `200 OK` on `/keepalive` and `200 OK` on unchanged `pl.m3u8` for **`165s` (`304` stall polls)** until returning `404 Not Found` at `08:13:57 EST` (followed by `16.9s` of `404` retries before `attempt=2` at `08:14:15 EST`).

## 6. Root Cause Technical Deep Dive

### 6.1 Unconditional `IdleGrace` Entry & Retention on Unhealthy Sessions
In [`Tablo4thGen.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/tuner/Tablo4thGen.scala#L1767-L1769), `SessionManager.Release` placed any `Live` session whose last client disconnected unconditionally into `IdleGrace` (`180s`) with a `Sink.ignore` drain (`enterIdleGrace`). While ideal for healthy channel surfing (`0ms` fast-attach), entering `IdleGrace` when `FreezeFramePacer` is active (`freezeActive`), cold retune recovery is underway (`inRecovery`), or the session has collapsed to `0%` feed quality keeps `ResilientHlsSource` retrying `POST /watch` indefinitely with zero viewers attached. Similarly, if a healthy `IdleGrace` session degrades into freeze pacing or cold retunes while sitting idle, `SessionManager` had no signal to end `IdleGrace` early.

### 6.2 Fast-Attach onto a Dead `IdleGrace` Session (13% Roku Hang)
In [`Tablo4thGen.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/tuner/Tablo4thGen.scala#L1738-L1749), `SessionManager.Acquire` matched `Some(idle: SessionState.IdleGrace)` without checking session health. When Roku re-tuned `CBS 4.1` at `08:13:28 EST`, `fast-attached` handed PMS a stream carrying only synthetic repeat P-frames and null packets (`signalQualityPct=0.0`). `AlignToIFrameStage` timed out after `3000ms` without ever seeing a live keyframe, causing Roku to hang at 13% for `94.2s` — whereas fresh `Opening` tunes on `FOX 25.1` and `NBC 15.1` properly received pre-roll keepalive during `retryInitialTune` and surfaced a clean PMS `Error 16` after `25.8s`.

### 6.3 Pekko `RestartSettings` Default `maxRestartsWithin` Resetting Backoff to `[1]`
In [`ResilientHlsSource.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala#L89), `RestartSettings(minBackoff = retryDelay, maxBackoff = effectiveMax, randomFactor = 0.2)` leaves `maxRestartsWithin` at Pekko's default of `minBackoff` (`1.second`). Inside Pekko's `RestartWithBackoffLogic`, a `"ResetRestartCount"` timer fires `maxRestartsWithin` after starting an inner attempt and resets `restartCount = 0`. Because Tablo takes `~6.1s` (`> 1s`) to return `503 no_signal_lock`, every failed cold retune ran past the `1s` reset window and logged `Restarting stream due to failure [1]`, pinning retry spacing at `1s` instead of backing off `1s → 2s → 4s → 8s → 15s`.

### 6.4 Unbounded Wait on Stale `200 OK` `pl.m3u8` Before First Cold Retune
Under `dea1c71`, `HlsPlaylistPoller.resolveStallOutcome` keeps the existing `/watch` session open indefinitely as long as `/keepalive` returns `200 OK` and `pl.m3u8` does not return `404` or `#EXT-X-ENDLIST`. When Tablo loses RF lock cleanly without immediately reaping the session, it can serve an unchanged `200 OK` `pl.m3u8` for `165s` (`304` stall polls) before returning `404`. Capping unchanged playlist stalls at a conservative `60s` (`STREAM_HLS_MAX_STALL_SEC=60`, `<= 0` disables) preserves sub-60s same-session RF fade recoveries (such as the `34.95s` `NBC 15.1` recovery on `2026-11-19`) while escalating dead playlists to `WatchSession.coldRetune` nearly 3x faster.

### 6.5 Client Observation — Roku `Streaming Stick 4K` (`3820X2`) Subtitles
Consistent with `2026.11.20_0` (`dev=3` `3810X`), PMS negotiated `subtitleDecision="ignore"` and passed `-sei:0 -a53_cc` to `h264_vaapi` for `dev=51` (`Streaming Stick 4K`, `3820X2`). Setting **Plex for Roku → Settings → Video → Burn Subtitles → Always** remains the client-side workaround for EIA-608 closed captions on Roku devices.

## 7. Architecture & Design Recommendations

1. **Fix `ResilientHlsSource` Backoff Reset Window**:
   - **Action**: Configure `RestartSettings` with `.withMaxRestarts(Int.MaxValue, (tuneTimeout + effectiveMax).max(retryDelay))` (default `35s`) so slow-failing retunes (`~6.1s` `503` or `20s` `tuneTimeout`) genuinely back off `1s → 2s → 4s → 8s → 15s`, while healthy streams running `>= 35s` automatically reset backoff to `1s`.
   - **Expected Outcome**: Eliminates flat `1s` retune hammering on Tablo during prolonged signal-lock outages.
2. **Cap Unchanged HLS Playlist Stalls (`STREAM_HLS_MAX_STALL_SEC=60`)**:
   - **Action**: Track `stallStartNanos` in `HlsPlaylistPoller.PollState` and fail `resolveStallOutcome` with `HlsError.PlaylistStalled(maxStallSec)` once an unchanged playlist stall reaches `STREAM_HLS_MAX_STALL_SEC` (default `60s`, `<= 0` disables).
   - **Expected Outcome**: Preserves `< 60s` same-session RF fade recoveries while cutting `165s` stale-playlist waits down to `60s`.
3. **Health-Gate `SessionManager` `IdleGrace` (`Release`, `Acquire`, & `SessionUnhealthy`)**:
   - **Action**: Expose `onFreezeStateChanged: Boolean => Unit` from `MpegTsSync.cacheFlow`, track `isHealthy()` (`!tornDown && !inRecovery && !isFrozen` + non-zero feed snapshot) on `TabloSessionMeta`, and send `Command.SessionUnhealthy` from `SessionRunner` on freeze activation or cold retune start:
     - `Release`: skip `IdleGrace` and tear down immediately when `!live.meta.isHealthy()`.
     - `Acquire` on `IdleGrace`: evict and open a fresh tune when `!idle.meta.isHealthy()`.
     - `SessionUnhealthy`: end `IdleGrace` immediately when an idle session degrades, while leaving `Live` sessions with active viewers untouched.
   - **Expected Outcome**: Eliminates unattended background retune storms and prevents 13% Roku hangs from fast-attaching onto dead sessions, with zero regression to healthy `0ms` `IdleGrace` fast-attach.
