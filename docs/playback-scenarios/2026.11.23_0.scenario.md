## 6. Root Cause Technical Deep Dive

### 6.1 Stale-Buffer Re-Emission Across `PlaylistStalled(60)` Cold Retunes
In [`Tablo4thGen.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/tuner/Tablo4thGen.scala#L2309-L2327), `SessionRunner`'s cold-recovery path unconditionally calls `lastSeqRef.set(0)` before invoking `streamFromWatchSession(..., isRecovery = true)`. When Tablo has not finished recycling its internal tuner buffer after `DELETE /player/sessions/{token}` (responding to the immediate `POST /watch` in `~131ms`), it hands back a new playlist token pointing at the **exact same frozen `segw.ts?...` file and trailing byte-ranges** (`seq=345..347` at `17:13:20Z` / `17:14:24Z`, `seq=955..957` at `17:58:08Z`). Because `lastSeqRef` was zeroed (and `ResilientHlsSource` injects `MPEGTS_DISCONTINUITY_PACKET`, resetting `MpegTsSync`'s `maxInputVideoPts` watermark), `HlsPlaylistPoller` treats those 3 already-emitted segments as fresh live-edge data — replaying `~7.5–9s` of past video, falsely breaking out of freeze pacing, incrementing `ccErrors` by `+1`, and resetting Pekko's `35s` backoff counter to `[1]`.

### 6.2 Late-Completing `POST /watch` After `20s` `StallWatchdog` Timeout
In [`ResilientHlsSource.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala#L102), `StallWatchdog` enforces `STREAM_TUNE_TIMEOUT_SEC` (default `20s`) on cold-retune startup. Under fringe RF lock (e.g., `17:15:47Z` `attempt=8`), Tablo's `POST /watch` took `23.8s` to acquire signal lock. When `StallWatchdog` cancelled the inner `Source.futureSource` at `20.25s`, the underlying Pekko HTTP Future was not cancelled; `3.56s` later (`17:16:10.908Z`), `.map` in [`Tablo4thGen.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/tuner/Tablo4thGen.scala#L2314-L2327) still executed `currentSession.set(newSession)` and `scheduleKeepalive()`, leaving a freshly locked Tablo session unstreamed for `12.5s` until Pekko's `[5]` `16s` backoff timer fired `attempt=9`.

## 7. Architecture & Design Recommendations

1. **Deduplicate Stale-Buffer Segment Keys Across Cold Retunes (High Value, Low Risk)**:
   - **Action**: Track `lastEmittedSegmentKeys: AtomicReference[Set[String]]` (using `HlsPlaylistPoller.segmentKey`, i.e., `seq|url|offset|length`) in `SessionRunner` ([`Tablo4thGen.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/tuner/Tablo4thGen.scala#L2171-L2327)) and seed `HlsPlaylistPoller.initial(...)` with prior emitted keys on cold recovery. When a cold retune's first playlist contains only already-emitted `(url, byteRange)` keys, suppress re-emission (keeping `FreezeFramePacer` seamlessly engaged and avoiding `~8s` visual replay loops) and optionally add a brief `1–2s` delay between `DELETE` and `POST /watch` when `closedByTablo == false` so Tablo finishes releasing its stale tuner buffer.
   - **Expected Outcome**: Eliminates `~8s` video replays during prolonged RF freezes and prevents stale cached segments from falsely resetting backoff to `[1]`.
2. **Harden Cold-Retune Timeout & Cancellation (`STREAM_TUNE_TIMEOUT_SEC` & Late-Future Guard — Medium Value, Low Risk)**:
   - **Action**: Raise default `STREAM_TUNE_TIMEOUT_SEC` from `20s` to `25–30s` (or add a dedicated cold-retune grace margin) so `21–24s` weak-signal Tablo locks succeed on the first attempt, and guard [`Tablo4thGen.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/tuner/Tablo4thGen.scala#L2314-L2327)'s `coldRetune` `.map` callback with a per-attempt cancellation flag (mirroring `retryInitialTune`) so a cancelled attempt never mutates `currentSession` / `keepaliveTask` after `StallWatchdog` fires.
   - **Expected Outcome**: Eliminates wasted `12–16s` extra blackout waits and prevents orphaned keepalive timers after tune timeouts.
3. **Operational — Expand Plex `/transcode` `tmpfs` Beyond `12G` (`24G` or NVMe Scratch)**:
   - **Action**: Increase the Plex container `/transcode` mount from `12G` (`66%` full at `4h 12m`, `~1.9 GB/hr` on `1080i` + `inlineass`) to `24G` (or NVMe-backed directory as documented in [`docs/USAGE.md`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/docs/USAGE.md)).
   - **Expected Outcome**: Extends continuous single-channel `1080i` headroom from `~6h` to `12h+` without hitting Failure Mode F (`No space left on device`).
4. **Minor Observability — Tag `404` Probes Distinctly in `HlsBackend` Logs**:
   - **Action**: Pass a short probe reason (`unchanged` vs. `404`) into the probe callback in [`HlsBackend.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsBackend.scala#L244-L258) so immediate `404` probes (`stallPolls=6..9, 13..15, 17..19`) are visually distinct from `sessionProbeMinPolls=12` unchanged-playlist probes in log triage.
5. **Keep Current Surgical `MpegTsSync` Sanitization Policy (No Over-Gating)**:
   - **Observation**: Although `179,124` live OTA `[mpeg2video]` errors occurred during wet-canopy / `19 kt` gusts (`TEI=0`), `0` crashes occurred and the viewer confirmed the visual experience (brief artifacting + clean freeze/resume) matches their target live-TV behavior. Avoid reverting to aggressive whole-GOP dropping (which increased blackout durations in `c57c238`).
