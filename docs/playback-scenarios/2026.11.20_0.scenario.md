## 6. Root Cause Technical Deep Dive

### 6.1 Failure Mode H — Upstream Tablo Audio Encoder Wedge & Retune Blind Spot
In [`MpegTsSync.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L784-L793), `checkCorruptPesTimestamp` (`isVideo == false`) correctly prevents broken/drifted audio timestamps (`|mappedPts − lastOutputPts| > 2.0s`) from corrupting downstream A/V sync. However, when Tablo's own hardware audio encoder wedges after an RF fade (`seq=4049/4050`):
1. Video packets (`PID 0x100`) keep arriving at `29.97 fps`, so neither `StallWatchdog`, `FreezeFramePacer`, nor `HlsPlaylistPoller` detects a stall.
2. Tablo's control-plane `POST /player/sessions/{token}/keepalive?lh=1` keeps returning `200 OK`, so `WatchSession.coldRetune` never triggers.
3. Because the upstream Tablo audio stream itself lost codec parameters and drifted `-214s` → `-415s` behind video, scrubbing audio packets alone cannot recover audio — only tearing down the wedged `/watch` session (`DELETE`) and opening a fresh `/watch` session (`POST`) resets the Tablo encoder.

### 6.2 Client & Infrastructure Findings (Outside Proxy Code)
- **Roku Closed Captions**: Setting **Plex for Roku → Settings → Video → Burn Subtitles → Always** switches Roku from `subtitleDecision="ignore"` (`-sei:0 -a53_cc`) to `subtitleDecision="burn"`, matching iPhone 8.
- **Roku Mid-Program Live Join & Seek Loop**: Driven by PMS launching Stage-2 at `-live_start_index 0` from the `10:00:00` airing offset (`offset=8860.93s`) plus `+37s` of Stage-1 timeline drift from the stale `2026-11-16` container's `ptsDeficitMs` freeze latch (fixed in `9a2dd6d`).
- **`/transcode` Tmpfs Sizing**: Dual-client long-duration Live TV consumed ~11.9 GB of the `12G` tmpfs (`96.77 MB` free); expanding `/transcode` to `24G` or NVMe/SSD avoids low-disk warnings and premature segment eviction.

## 7. Architecture & Design Recommendations

1. **Sustained-Audio-Loss Automatic Retune Trigger (Failure Mode H)**:
   - Track active non-frozen live video duration alongside primary-PID (`audioPids.headOption`) clean vs. dropped/scrubbed audio frames in [`MpegTsSync.cacheFlow`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala).
   - When `>= STREAM_AUDIO_LOSS_TIMEOUT_SEC` (default `30s`, `<= 0` disables) of active non-frozen live video (`>= 150` live video frames) elapses with zero recovered primary audio, fire a single-shot inner-stream abort via `ResilientHlsSource.RetuneTrigger` (`HlsError.SustainedAudioLoss`) so `WatchSession.coldRetune` executes `DELETE` + fresh `POST /watch` without dropping downstream Plex clients.
   - Guard against false positives during freezes/replays (`onPause`), video-only channels (`hasPrimaryAudioPid && (seenCleanLiveAudio || droppedAudioFrames > 0)`), secondary SAP track errors (primary PID scope), and broadcaster-side audio outages (exponential backoff `30s → 60s → 120s → 240s → 300s` cap, reset after `100` clean live audio frames).
2. **Operational Deployment**:
   - Rebuild and redeploy `tablo2hdhomerun:latest` from `main` to pick up `255d354`..`8d6b716` and the Failure Mode H retune fix.
   - Set **Burn Subtitles = Always** in Plex for Roku and expand Plex's `/transcode` tmpfs beyond `12G` for multi-client all-day streaming.
