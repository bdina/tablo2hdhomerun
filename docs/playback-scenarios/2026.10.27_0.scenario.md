### 5.3 Accounting of the "Few Freezes": Upstream Pacing Gaps & Decoder Errors

#### 1. Peak Upstream Delivery Gap at 17:46:01 EDT (21:46:01 UTC):
```text
2026-10-27 21:46:01.974 INFO [stream:hls] segment completed label=ee905255 seq=1673 duration=0.0s bytes=9045244 gapMs=6526
```
The Tablo delayed segment 1673 by 6,526ms and delivered a double-sized 9.0 MB payload. Under previous watchdog thresholds (4s soft / 8s hard), this gap or subsequent jitter would frequently trigger premature cold retunes. Under the 45s session retention architecture, the proxy held the lease steady, allowing downstream buffers to catch up.

## 6. Technical Deep Dive: Why the Architecture Succeeded

### 6.1 Decoupled Audio Alignment Eliminates Failure Mode G
In commit [`d14956d`](https://github.com/bdina/tablo2hdhomerun/commit/d14956d), `AlignToIFrameStage` was refactored in [`MpegTsSync.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L644) so that audio packets are **never dropped while awaiting a video keyframe**.
When PMS attached to the live broadcast hub:
1. Audio packets on PID 257 arriving with `PUSI=1` were passed through immediately to `outputBuilder`.
2. Audio and null packets were explicitly excluded from the `droppedPackets` counter.
3. FFmpeg's `avformat_find_stream_info` probe buffer received both video headers (PAT/PMT) and valid AAC audio PES frames during the initial 1.5 MB read window.
4. PMS confirmed `sourceAudioCodec="aac"` and set `audioDecision="copy"`. Audio played in perfect lip-sync from the first second of playback.

### 6.2 Native Hardware STC Timestamp Preservation (B-Frame Safe)
In commit [`7db3318`](https://github.com/bdina/tablo2hdhomerun/commit/7db3318), `MpegTsSync.cacheFlow` was updated to preserve Tablo's native hardware System Time Clock (`PTS`, `DTS`, and `PCR`) timestamps untouched by default (`DefaultFreezeThreshold = Duration.Zero`).
- **The B-Frame Pitfall in MPEG-2**: In broadcast TV, B-frames are transmitted in decode (`DTS`) order rather than presentation (`PTS`) order. Consequently, B-frame PTS values are naturally lower than the preceding anchor I/P frame (`diffFromOut < 0`).
- **Preservation Benefit**: Under previous synthetic rebasing logic, negative diffs triggered artificial timestamp shifts (`ptsDeltaTicks`), scrambling B-frame display order and accelerating the audio clock relative to video. Preserving native hardware STC timestamps eliminated clock drift entirely, allowing the stream to run for nearly 4 hours without lip-sync degradation.

### 6.3 45-Second Session Stall Retention Rides Out Packager Jitter
The Tablo 4th Gen packager occasionally produces delivery jitter where segment intervals stretch to 4.0–6.5s (such as seq 1673 at 6,526ms).
Under the updated [`HlsPlaylistPoller.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsPlaylistPoller.scala) and [`ResilientHlsSource.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala):
- The proxy holds the `/watch` session open for up to `45s` (`STREAM_STALL_TIMEOUT_SEC = 45`), dropping to `500ms` fast polling when segments are delayed.
- Inter-segment delivery pauses emit benign `0x1FFF` null packets to keep downstream TCP sockets alive while decoders hold the previous frame.
- The proxy never dropped tuner lease `ee905255` across the entire 4-hour test.

### 6.4 Surgical Slice Dropping & Clamped Slice Bounds Protect VAAPI Decoders
When RF bit errors occurred (triggering the 21 packet submission errors in PMS):
- Rather than discarding the entire GOP (which causes multi-second video blackouts and player buffer starvation), `MpegTsSync.cacheFlow` surgically dropped only the damaged slice (`droppingVideoSlice`) on `TEI` or out-of-bounds start codes (`code > maxSliceRow`).
- Slice row indices were clamped to `<= maxSliceRow`, preventing FFmpeg `slice below image` fatal asserts.
- Outbound Continuity Counters (`CC`) were continuously rewritten per PID, ensuring downstream demuxers saw unbroken continuity counter sequences.
- Downstream hardware VAAPI decoders absorbed the transient macroblock dropouts in <100ms and resumed smooth playback without crashing.

## 7. Architecture & Operational Recommendations

### 1. Host Transcode Ramdisk Sizing for All-Day TV Playback
- **Observation**: At `20:00:26 EDT` (~2.7 hours into continuous streaming), Plex Media Server logged multiple warnings:
  ```text
  WARN - Low disk space: 0B source file, 1GB capacity, 96-99MB available on "/transcode/Transcode/Sessions/plex-transcode-..."
  ```
