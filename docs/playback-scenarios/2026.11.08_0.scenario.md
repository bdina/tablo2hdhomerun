## 2. Executive Summary of Incident

### Scenario Classification
- **Classification**: **Mixed / Multi-Channel Benchmark**
- **Primary Objective**: Stress-test stream stability and failure handling across three broadcast profiles (CBS 4.1, NBC 15.1, ABC 9.1).
- **Failure Mode**: Mode B (Initial Signal Lock 503 Timeout), Mode E (Segment Delivery Jitter Starvation), Mode F (Transcoder tmpfs Storage Low)
- **Key Outcome**: CBS lock failure timed out PMS at 25s; NBC delivery jitter starved Roku buffer into 121s hang; ABC 2.5h stream hit 10GB tmpfs low disk space warnings; validates MPEG-2 slice clamping (88 clamped slices), 180s idle-grace fast-attach, and `FreezeFramePacer` 46s bridge.
- **Validation Status**: Multi-channel benchmark (`d09cc2f`); validates MPEG-2 slice clamping (88 clamped slices), 180s idle-grace fast-attach, and `FreezeFramePacer` 46s bridge

On **November 8, 2026**, a series of empirical tests were conducted during the morning testing window (**07:05 – 10:13 EST** / **12:05 – 15:13 UTC**) to stress-test stream stability and failure handling across multiple channels. The tests revealed three distinct failure modes spanning the entire broadcast-to-display pipeline:

1. **Initial Demodulator Signal Lock Failure (CBS 4.1 at 07:05 EST)**:
   A tune request to fringe station WBZ-TV immediately failed at the Tablo tuner hardware (`503 no_signal_lock: Unable to tune or get signal lock`). While `tablo2hdhomerun` scheduled an automatic retry in 2s, Plex Media Server's internal media grabber timeout expired after 25 seconds, terminating the session with `Recorder: Error 16 (Recording failed. Please check your tuner or antenna.)`.

2. **Upstream Segment Jitter & Downstream Buffer Starvation (NBC 15.1 at 07:06 – 07:10 EST)**:
   Tuning WBTS-CD succeeded (`leaseId=6440dc9a`), and direct-stream playback began on the Roku player. However, upstream ATSC reception instability induced severe HLS segment delivery gaps (`gapMs=7237`, `gapMs=11388`, `gapMs=4008`, `duration=13.7s`). Although `MpegTsSync` actively sanitized corrupt packets (clamping 88 out-of-bounds MPEG-2 slice rows), the delivery jitter starved the downstream Roku client buffer. After 38 seconds of playback, the Roku player entered a **121-second continuous buffering freeze** (from `t=38.5s` to `t=159.9s`) before the Roku player gave up and aborted (`state="stopped" progress="0"`). A subsequent re-tune fast-attached via 180s `idle-grace`, but hit a complete 46.3s upstream video stall (absorbed by `FreezeFramePacer`), prompting another client abort.

3. **2.5-Hour Soak Test Termination: Transcoder Storage Exhaustion & Upstream HLS Stalls (ABC 9.1 at 07:38 – 10:13 EST)**:
   A long-duration soak test on local primary station WMUR-TV streamed flawlessly for **2 hours, 31 minutes (9,091 seconds)**, transferring **14.31 GB** across 4,341 HLS segments with zero slice anomalies. However, after ~2 hours of continuous streaming:
   - **Plex Transcoder tmpfs Full**: PMS accumulates live TV transcode chunks in `/transcode`. Because `/transcode` was mounted on a `10G` tmpfs ramdisk, free space plummeted below 100 MB (~98.68 MB available out of 10 GB), triggering an avalanche of `WARN - Low disk space` entries in PMS.
   - **Upstream Tablo HLS Playlist Stalls**: Concurrently, the Tablo 4th Gen HLS packager began stalling, failing to publish new playlist sequences (`playlist stalled, probing session liveness` tripped 8 times over 22 minutes).
   - **Termination**: At 10:09:31 EST (9,079s mark in Plex), the downstream player stalled and disconnected (`outcome=downstream-closed`). `tablo2hdhomerun` preserved the lease through 180s `idle-grace` and tore down cleanly at 10:12:31 EST.

### Key Session Metrics:
- **Total Observed Window**: 3 hours, 7 minutes (07:05:19 – 10:12:31 EST / 12:05:19 – 15:12:31 UTC)
- **Channels Evaluated**: CBS 4.1 (`S20431_004_01`), NBC 15.1 (`S91446_015_01`), ABC 9.1 (`S19604_009_01`)

- **Fast-Attach Latency from Idle-Grace**: 0 ms attach, 1.7s I-frame alignment (NBC 15.1 re-tune)

### Environmental & Meteorological Summary:
- Surface observations at **KMHT** (Manchester-Boston Regional Airport, NH) recorded sustained winds of **8–15 knots (9–17 mph)** with repeated peak gusts of **20–26 knots (23–30 mph)** out of **280°–310° (WNW/NW)** under broken cloud decks (2,200–3,400 ft).
- **RF Propagation Impact**: The 23–30 mph wind gusts agitated foliage and induced mechanical antenna mast vibration across the 48-mile fringe path to Needham, MA, directly causing the ATSC 1.0 signal lock failure on CBS (RF 20) and the severe segment delivery jitter and packet dropouts on NBC (RF 32). In contrast, the 12-mile local path to ABC (RF 9, Mt. Uncanoonuc) remained robust against gust multipath fading.

## 6. Root Cause Technical Deep Dive

### 1. ATSC Demodulator Carrier Acquisition Failure & Plex Grabber 25s Timeout
On fringe station WBZ-TV (CBS 4.1, UHF RF 20, ~48 miles), ambient wind gusts (peak 26–30 kt) agitated tree canopies along the line-of-sight. The Tablo tuner hardware's ATSC 1.0 demodulator was unable to acquire an 8VSB carrier lock within its initial acquisition window, returning `503 {"error":{"code":"playback_failed","details":"no_signal_lock"}}`.
`tablo2hdhomerun` correctly scheduled an automated retry 2 seconds later. However, **Plex Media Server enforces an unconfigurable 25-second timeout on its media grabber**. Because the Tablo tuner's second lock attempt had not completed by `t=25.0s`, PMS terminated the request with `Recorder: Error 16 (Recording failed. Please check your tuner or antenna.)` and tore down the TCP connection.

### 2. Upstream Delivery Jitter Induces Player Buffer Depletion & 121s Hang
On NBC 15.1, the Tablo locked carrier, but fringe RF instability caused the Tablo HLS packager to emit segments irregularly. Rather than the expected steady cadence of 2.4s segments arriving every ~2.4s, segments arrived with inter-arrival gaps stretching up to **11.4 seconds (`gapMs=11388`)** and segment durations stretching to **13.68 seconds**.
Although `MpegTsSync` clamped 88 damaged MPEG-2 slices, preventing decoder syntax crashes, the physical byte delivery rate fell below the real-time bitrate. The downstream Roku player depleted its local jitter buffer at `t=38.5s` and stalled. Because Plex continued holding the HTTP connection open, the Roku player entered a **121-second buffering freeze** before its internal player timeout forced a stop (`state="stopped"` at `t=159.9s`).

### 3. Downstream Transcoder Ramdisk Exhaustion on Long-Duration Streams
During the 2.5-hour continuous stream on ABC 9.1 (14.31 GB transferred), PMS operated in direct-stream mode (`videoDecision="copy" audioDecision="copy"`), remuxing the MPEG-TS stream into live HLS/DASH fragments. PMS writes these active session chunks to the directory configured for transcoding (`/transcode/Transcode/Sessions/...`).
In this Docker environment, `/transcode` was mounted on a **`10G` tmpfs memory-backed filesystem**. Because Plex retains a large rolling window of chunks for live seekback, after ~2 hours of 15–19 Mbps broadcast ingest, the volume accumulated over 9.9 GB of data. Available disk space dropped to **98.68 MB**, causing PMS to emit high-frequency `Low disk space` warnings. Under this storage constraint and concurrent upstream HLS playlist stalls, downstream playback terminated.

## 7. Architecture & Design Recommendations

### 1. Pre-Roll Pacing for Slow Hardware Tune Locks
- **Problem**: When the Tablo demodulator requires multiple retries to lock a fringe channel, Plex times out at 25 seconds and aborts the stream before the tuner completes acquisition.
- **Action**: Enhance `SessionManager` and `ChannelActor` to begin emitting synthetic ATSC PAT/PMT and pacing `0x1FFF` null packets or freeze frames to downstream HTTP clients immediately upon receiving the initial tune request.
- **Expected Outcome**: Plex's downstream HTTP reader sees continuous active traffic and valid transport framing from `t=0`, preventing the 25-second grabber timeout from firing while the Tablo hardware completes secondary carrier acquisition.

### 2. Sizing and Retention Policies for `/transcode` Ramdisks
- **Problem**: Long-duration live TV streaming (>2 hours) fills a `10G` tmpfs transcoder partition, leading to `Low disk space` warnings and stream instability.
- **Action**:
  1. Increase the `/transcode` tmpfs volume size in the Plex container configuration from `10G` to `24G` or `32G` (as documented in [`USAGE.md`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/docs/USAGE.md) for 24/7 streaming).
  2. Configure Plex's **Transcoder default throttle buffer** and verify Plex's segment retention settings to ensure older live fragments are pruned aggressively during multi-hour live playback.

### 3. Jitter Buffer Metric Logging in `ResilientHlsSource`
- **Problem**: When upstream segment delivery suffers extreme inter-arrival jitter (`gapMs > 5000`), downstream players starve before the proxy logs an explicit stall.
- **Action**: Add an operational log metric tracking the moving-average segment delivery ratio (`segmentDuration / gapMs`). When the delivery ratio drops below `1.0` for more than 3 consecutive segments, log a `WARN [stream:hls] delivery underrun risk` line.
- **Expected Outcome**: Immediate operational visibility into upstream delivery jitter before downstream player buffers deplete.
