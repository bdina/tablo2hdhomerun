## 1. Executive Summary & Environment

### Executive Summary
Following the `2026-11-11 10:11:28 EST` (`15:11:28 UTC`) container rebuild running proxy commit `7a0e263` (`v0.0.17`), a manual live TV test was conducted on the Roku client (`dev=3: Roku (Basement)` in Plex SQLite `devices`) starting with **Plex Roku Direct Stream enabled** and subsequently **switching the Roku client back to the Plex Media Server (PMS) transcoder**.

The session establishes an unambiguous empirical contrast between the two client playback modes across identical hardware, weather, proxy image (`7a0e263`), and upstream RF channels:

1. **Phase 1 — Roku Direct Stream Instability (`10:29:48 – 11:08:25 EST`, Attaches #1–#7)**:
   - Produced **7 consecutive rapid `downstream-closed` client aborts** spanning `28.03s` to `357.82s` (`5m 58s`) across both `COZITV 15.2` (4 attaches) and `CBS 4.1` (3 attaches).
   - Most critically, **Attach #1** (`COZITV 15.2`, `clientId=e04d9561`) aborted cleanly at `74.78s` with **`signal=100%`, `feed=100%`, `sliceDrops=0`, `ptsScrubs=0`, and `freezeAct=0`**, proving that the Roku Direct Stream container/demuxer dropped playback even against a faultless upstream MPEG-2 transport stream.
   - Downstream attaches under Direct Stream terminated either immediately after upstream jitter (`47ms` after resume on Attach #2; `2.08s` after resume/anomalies on Attach #4) or mid-freeze-frame (`2.76s` into blackout on Attach #3; `5.40s` into blackout on Attach #7).
2. **Phase 2 — Switch Back to PMS Transcoder & Immediate Stabilization (`11:09:34 – 13:11:59 EST`, Attaches #8–#9)**:
   - Beginning either at **Attach #8 (`11:09:34 EST`, `CBS 4.1`)** or **Attach #9 (`11:28:08 EST`, `COZITV 15.2`)** *(exact toggle minute inferred between Attaches #7–#8 or #8–#9 because `2026-11-11` PMS logs rotated off; see Section 3)*, channel stability recovered completely despite **far harsher RF and upstream transport disruptions** than Phase 1 ever encountered.
   - **Attach #8 (`CBS 4.1`, `18m 35s`)** survived a forced `pusi=true` I-Frame timeout fallback (`3120ms` video wait), four `404 Not Found` upstream segment errors triggering a `cold recovery retune` (`49.9s` blackout), a `76.8s` freeze-frame blackout, two further `16.5s`/`38.4s` blackouts, and two short upstream HTTP reads — closing only `0.80s` after the user manually switched to `COZITV 15.2`.
   - **Attach #9 (`COZITV 15.2`, `1h 43m 52s`)** ran uninterrupted until client close (`13:11:59 EST`, followed by `180s` idle-grace teardown `L5061`), effortlessly bridging `avSkewMs=-1986ms`, `767` PTS scrubs in a single window, `107` `FreezeFramePacer` activations (`1,611` bursts), and a `43.5s` complete upstream blackout.
3. **PMS SQLite Bandwidth Confirmation**:
   - At `10:00 EST` (Direct Stream phase), PMS outbound bytes (`257.2 MB`, `2.838 Mbps`) were **`0.905x`** of proxy client egress (`284.2 MB`), consistent with raw Direct Stream passthrough (`0.980x` observed on `2026-11-08` `dev=51`).
   - At `12:00 EST` (pure transcoder phase on `COZITV 15.2` lease `992c03f1`), PMS delivered `938.6 MB` (`2.092 Mbps` over `3590s`) vs. `802.0 MB` (`1.784 Mbps`) upstream proxy ingest (**`+17.0%` / `1.17x`**), matching confirmed PMS Intel VAAPI `h264` transcode overhead (`+24.8%` on `2026-11-14` `dev=51`).

## 7. Root Cause Technical Deep Dive & Recommendations

### 1. Why Roku Direct Stream Failed Even on Clean MPEG-2 Streams (`Attaches #1–#7`)
- **Zero-error failure proves client-side intolerance, not proxy data corruption**: Attach #1 (`COZITV 15.2`, `e04d9561`) closed after `74.78s` with `signal=100.0%`, `feed=100.0%`, `tei=0`, `cc=0`, `sliceDrops=0`, `ptsScrubs=0`, and `freezeAct=0` ([StreamMetrics.scala](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/StreamMetrics.scala#L123-L151)). Because the PMS tuner pipeline in Direct Stream mode forwards remuxed broadcast MPEG-2 elementary streams (Stage-2 XML was not logged due to PMS log rotation; remux/passthrough is inferred from Direct Stream mode and the `0.905x` PMS-to-proxy byte ratio at `10:00 EST`), the Roku native MPEG-2/AC-3 Direct Stream pipeline receives variable OTA broadcast GOP structures, interlaced signaling, and raw Tablo `.ts` segment pacing untouched by an encoder.
- **Zero jitter tolerance during `FreezeFramePacer` bridging**: Even though `tablo2hdhomerun` injects synthetically paced valid frames via [`FreezeFramePacer`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L1150-L1240) and reports [`real data resumed`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala#L314) cleanly to PMS, Direct Stream Roku sessions dropped either mid-freeze (`2.76s` into blackout on #3; `5.40s` on #7) or within milliseconds/seconds of real-stream resumption (`47ms` on #2; `2.08s` on #4).

### 2. Why PMS Transcoding Eliminated Channel Instability (`Attaches #8–#9`)
- **Bitstream & timeline sandboxing via VAAPI H.264**: Switching Roku back to PMS transcoding inserts FFmpeg/Plex Transcoder (`mpeg2video` decode + VAAPI `h264` encode + `aac` audio encode) between the raw proxy MPEG-2 TS and the Roku HLS player. PMS absorbs:
  1. **Forced PUSI/non-IDR join boundaries**: Attach #8 hit the 3s [`DefaultMaxAlignmentWait`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L1400-L1469) threshold (`3120ms video wait, 3211ms total, 289 dropped packets`) and entered the forced `pusi=true` fallback ([MpegTsSync.scala](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L1550-L1620)) — PMS's decoder waited cleanly for the next reference frame instead of tearing down playback.
  2. **Extreme upstream blackouts & cold retunes**: Attach #8 survived a `49.9s` cold retune blackout after four upstream `404`s and a `76.8s` freeze blackout, while Attach #9 survived a `43.5s` blackout and `avSkewMs=-1986ms` / `767` PTS scrubs without ever closing the HTTP tuner stream.
  3. **Roku player simplicity**: The Roku receives clean, closed-GOP progressive H.264/AAC HLS segments buffered inside PMS `/transcode` rather than bare broadcast MPEG-2.

### 3. Explaining `stallPolls=2548` on Healthy Lease `992c03f1` (`Attach #9`)
Although `992c03f1` logged `stallPolls=2548` across `213` metric windows (`~12/window` before `seq=3006`, rising to `~24/window` after `12:23:56 EST`), this was **not** 2,548 channel freezes:
- In [`HlsPlaylistPoller.pollDelayMs`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsPlaylistPoller.scala#L101-L138), when `!playlistAdvanced` occurs on a poll, the poller drops its interval down to a `500ms` floor while Tablo publishes ~2s HLS segments (~`1.07s` around `seq=3006`).
- Every unchanged playlist poll triggers `next.stallPolls > state.stallPolls` and increments [`onStallPoll()`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsBackend.scala#L274-L275).
- By design in [`shouldProbeSession`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsPlaylistPoller.scala#L1-L100) and [`HlsBackend`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsBackend.scala#L256-L261), liveness probes only fire at `stallPolls >= 4` (every 4th poll, i.e., real stalls >= ~2–3s). Across the entire `1h 43m 52s` run, real probes fired only **`94` times**, confirming that `992c03f1` was predominantly healthy pre-publish polling plus genuine isolated freeze intervals (`107` activations, `43.5s` peak).

### Recommendations
1. **Operational Guidance — Keep Roku Plex Direct Stream Disabled for Live TV**: For Roku clients consuming OTA MPEG-2 via `tablo2hdhomerun` + Plex, leave **Direct Stream disabled** so PMS transcodes MPEG-2/AC-3 to H.264/AAC. Direct Stream saves PMS CPU/bandwidth overhead (`0.905x` vs `+17–25%`) at the cost of frequent `28s–358s` disconnects on Roku, even with `100%` upstream signal quality. *(Meanwhile, `Apple TV` `dev=7` ran clean evening control sessions across `NBC 15.1`, `ABC 9.1`, and `COZITV 15.2`; Stage-1 grabber logged `copy/copy`, while Stage-2 client delivery XML was not logged.)*
2. **Metrics Clarity (Future Code Improvement)**: Separate **benign pre-publish unchanged polls** (`stallPolls < 4`) from **verified stall polls** (`stallPolls >= 4`) in [`HlsBackend.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/HlsBackend.scala#L256-L291) and [`StreamMetrics.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/StreamMetrics.scala#L123-L151) so healthy long-running `~1–2s` HLS channels do not report inflated `stallPolls` totals (`2,548` vs `94` actual probes).
3. **PMS Log Diagnostics**: If repeating a controlled Direct Stream comparison, capture `/transcode` `Plex Transcoder Statistics.log` and `Plex Media Server.log` immediately at test end before unrelated overnight `mpeg2video` decode storms rotate the `11 MB` log ring buffers.
