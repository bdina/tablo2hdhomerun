## 2. Executive Summary of Incident

On the evening of **`2026-10-15`**, an extended viewing session streaming CBS Channel 4.1 (`S20431_004_01`) ran for **1 hour 50 minutes 20 seconds** (`19:53:52 EDT` – `21:44:12 EDT` / `23:53:52 UTC` – `01:44:12 UTC`). This run represents a crucial architectural milestone: **the first empirical field validation of commit [`tablo2hdhomerun:5f8c22d`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala)**, which expanded `idleGraceSec` from 75 seconds to 180 seconds (3 minutes), resolved soft watchdog scoping, raised stall limits to 8 seconds, and implemented client stream tracking and LRU eviction.

### Key Architectural Validations Observed:
1. **180-Second Idle-Grace Hand-Off**: When the iPad client disconnected at `20:58:37 EDT` (`00:58:45 UTC`), proxy entered a 180-second idle grace holding hardware lease `b41a81b0`. Exactly **28 seconds into the grace window** (`20:59:13 EDT`), Apple TV requested CBS 4.1. The proxy canceled idle grace, reused the live lease, and fast-attached Apple TV seamlessly ([`MpegTsSync.scala:L408-L534`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L408-L534)) without tearing down the tuner or incurring an upstream cold-tune delay.
2. **Concurrent Multi-Client Tuner Sharing**: From `20:36:51 EDT` to `20:58:37 EDT` (21m 46s), both iPad and Apple TV streamed concurrently from a **single upstream PMS live grabber connection (`0be5667a`)** against `tablo2hdhomerun`. PMS successfully multiplexed the raw ATSC MPEG-TS stream into two parallel VAAPI transcode pipelines (`e4a87442` and `10db9fc9`).
3. **Flap-Dampened Watchdog Recovery**: When Tablo experienced an ATSC demodulator stall at `20:57:47 EDT` (`00:57:47 UTC`), the proxy executed a 4-attempt recovery sequence combining soft retunes, cold retunes, and capped flap delays, successfully re-synchronizing to an I-frame on PID 256.
4. **Identification of Failure Mode E (Massive Silent RF Packet Corruption Burst)**: At `21:40:00`–`21:40:31 EDT`, Tablo suffered severe RF packet degradation (`gapMs=4524` on seq 2827). Tablo continued to send corrupt fragments, blinding the zero-byte proxy watchdog. Downstream, Plex logged **5,541 lines of `mpeg2video` decoding errors** (`overread`, `slice mismatch`, `invalid cbp -1`, `slice below image`). The transcoder survived without crashing, but corrupted GOP state starved the Apple TV player buffer (`progress=2474855`), leading the user to terminate playback at `21:41:12 EDT`.

### Summary of Failure Progression:
1. **Initial Baseline (`19:53:52 EDT`)**: Clean cold tune on CBS 4.1; iPad attaches and transcodes to 1080p H.264 via VAAPI driver `ivd` for Intel HD Graphics 530.
2. **Concurrent Client Attach (`20:36:51 EDT`)**: Apple TV tunes CBS 4.1; PMS reuses the existing live TV grabber session `134e61bd`, spawning secondary transcode instance `10db9fc9`. Both devices stream simultaneously for 21 minutes.
3. **Upstream Demodulator Stall (`20:57:47 EDT`)**: Tablo ceases producing HLS segments for 8 seconds. Watchdog trips; soft retune attempt 2 produces no data within 4s; cold retune attempt 3 forces fresh hardware session in 16.0s; short read on seq 1744 triggers attempt 4 soft recovery (resumes in 10.5s).
4. **Downstream Audio Decoder Glitch (`20:58:06 EDT`)**: Resumed stream contains damaged AAC headers (`AAC bitstream not in ADTS format and extradata missing`). iPad player progress freezes at `3822868ms` and terminates at `20:58:37 EDT`.
5. **Idle-Grace Rescue (`20:59:13 EDT`)**: Proxy enters 180s idle grace. 28 seconds later, Apple TV re-tunes CBS 4.1. Proxy cancels idle grace, fast-attaches Apple TV to lease `b41a81b0`, drops 30 leading packets in 1849ms, and locks PID 256 cleanly.
6. **Severe Silent Bitstream Corruption Burst (`21:40:00–21:40:31 EDT`)**: Tablo delivers segment seq 2827 with a 4,524ms gap and extensive bitstream corruption. Over 31 seconds, Plex transcoder absorbs **5,541 lines of `mpeg2video` decoding errors** (`overread`, `slice mismatch`, `invalid cbp -1`). Player buffer starves (`progress=2474855`), prompting user disconnect at `21:41:12 EDT`.
7. **Clean Session Teardown (`21:44:12 EDT`)**: Proxy holds lease in idle grace for exactly 180 seconds; timer cleanly fires and tears down Tablo hardware session.

## 6. Root Cause Technical Deep Dive

### 6.1 Architectural Validation of Commit `5f8c22d`

Commit [`tablo2hdhomerun:5f8c22d`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala) deployed four major resiliency mechanisms:
1. **180-Second Idle-Grace Expansion**: Previous builds utilized a 75-second grace window (`idleGraceSec = 75`), which was vulnerable to premature tuner release during pauses, app reloads, or cross-room device hand-offs. At `20:58:45 EDT`, when the iPad terminated, the proxy armed the 180s timer. When Apple TV connected **28 seconds later**, the lease was intact. By preventing an unnecessary cold tune, the proxy spared the Tablo tuner hardware from thrashing, preserved demodulator lock, and delivered an instantaneous fast-attach.
2. **Soft Watchdog Scoping Fix**: Prior to `5f8c22d`, the soft watchdog timer could misinterpret transient HLS segment fetch pauses as initial tune failures. Commit `5f8c22d` isolated the 4.0s tune timeout specifically to pre-data initialization ([`ResilientHlsSource.scala:L127-L133`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala#L127-L133)), allowing legitimate in-flight segment gaps to be governed cleanly by the 8.0s hard stall timeout.
3. **Flap Dampening Cap (4s)**: During the clustered stall at `20:57:47–20:58:05 EDT`, consecutive flaps were recorded. Instead of runaway exponential backoff (which previously delayed recovery by 15–30s), the flap penalty was clamped to 4 seconds, enabling Attempt 4 to recover data in 10.47 seconds.
4. **Client Stream Tracking & Accounting**: Method `trackClientStream` ([`Tablo4thGen.scala:L1915-L1950`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/tuner/Tablo4thGen.scala#L1915-L1950)) operationalized byte counts, session durations, and disconnect outcomes (`downstream-closed`), verifying that Session 1 delivered 6,132,118,764 bytes over 64m53s and Session 2 delivered 3,981,351,576 bytes over 41m58s.

### 6.2 Failure Mode E: Silent RF Packet Corruption & GOP Decoder Buffer Starvation

While commit `5f8c22d` successfully solved watchdog scoping and lease retention, this session exposed a severe instance of **Failure Mode E (Silent RF Packet Corruption)**:

#### 1. Calm Surface Weather vs. RF Propagation Fades
Surface observations at KMHT confirmed 0 mph wind, 0 gusts, and clear skies. Under calm, clear autumn nights, strong ground-level thermal inversions frequently develop across suburban New England river valleys. These atmospheric density boundaries create tropospheric ducting and dynamic multipath phase shifts over the 45-mile line-of-sight path from Needham, MA into Southern New Hampshire. 

#### 2. The Zero-Byte Watchdog Blind Spot
At `21:40:00–21:40:31 EDT`, the Tablo hardware did not drop carrier lock completely (which would have yielded zero bytes and tripped the 8s stall watchdog). Instead, the Tablo ATSC 8VSB demodulator continued demodulating damaged frames with uncorrectable Reed-Solomon errors, packaging corrupt MPEG-TS packets into HLS segments and serving them over HTTP:
- Segment seq 2827 arrived with a 4,524ms gap (`gapMs=4524`).
- Because bytes were actively arriving, the proxy watchdog evaluated the stream as healthy.
- The proxy forwarded these damaged transport packets directly downstream to Plex Media Server.

#### 3. Plex Transcoder Breakdown Under Packet Defect Flood
Plex's FFmpeg transcoder was overwhelmed by damaged bitstream headers:
- **`overread [1-9]`**: Bitstream reader attempted to parse variable-length DCT coefficients beyond the slice boundary.
- **`slice mismatch` & `invalid cbp -1`**: Macroblock geometry was corrupted, rendering subsequent P-frames and B-frames undecodable.
- **Audio Demuxer Glitch**: Damaged ADTS synchronization words (`AAC bitstream not in ADTS format`) broke audio packet cadence.

Although PMS's Intel HD Graphics 530 VAAPI hardware pipeline prevented a fatal segmentation fault (0 transcode runner deaths), the transcoder was unable to emit coherent H.264 video chunks to the Apple TV player. The player progress froze at `2474855ms`, leaving the screen black/frozen until the user manually stopped playback.

## 7. Architecture & Design Recommendations

### 1. In-Line ATSC Transport Error Indicator (TEI) & Continuity Counter Monitoring
- **Action**: Implement transport packet validation inside [`MpegTsSync.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala). Specifically:
  - Inspect byte 1 of each 188-byte packet for the `transport_error_indicator` (TEI) bit (`0x80`).
  - Track the 4-bit `continuity_counter` per PID (Video PID 256, Audio PID 257) to detect dropped transport packets.
  - Log metric lines: `WARN [stream:ts] corruption detected teiPackets=N ccErrors=M window=1000pkts`.
- **Expected Outcome**: Eliminates the watchdog blind spot by detecting physical RF packet corruption in real time, even when the Tablo continues trickling bytes.

### 2. Proactive Soft-Retune on Sustained Bitstream Defect Density
- **Action**: In [`ResilientHlsSource.scala`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala), introduce a defect-density trigger: if TEI packets exceed 5% of total packets or CC errors exceed 10 across a 2-second sliding window, fail the stage with `CorruptStreamException` to trigger an immediate soft retune.
- **Expected Outcome**: Proactively retunes the Tablo demodulator before thousands of corrupted macroblocks poison the downstream Plex transcode buffer.

### 3. Audio PES & ADTS Header Validation
- **Action**: Before releasing audio packets to downstream clients, verify that AAC packets contain valid 12-bit ADTS synchronization words (`0xFFF`). Replace corrupted audio frames with silent AAC frames.
- **Expected Outcome**: Prevents downstream players (such as the iPad at 20:58 EDT) from encountering fatal audio demuxer stalls (`AAC bitstream not in ADTS format`).
