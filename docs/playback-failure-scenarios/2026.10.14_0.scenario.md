## 2. Executive Summary of Incident

On the evening of **`2026-10-14`**, an extended viewing session streaming CBS Channel 4.1 (`S20431_004_01`) ran for **1 hour 45 minutes 16 seconds** (`19:50:20 EDT` – `21:35:36 EDT` / `23:50:20 UTC` – `01:35:36 UTC`). This run represents a major architectural milestone: **the first recorded multi-hour viewing session that held a single continuous Tablo hardware lease (`1c7188b6`) across 13 consecutive auto-retunes without crashing the downstream transcode runner or dropping the client stream socket**.

Crucially, this session served as the empirical validation test for commit [`tablo2hdhomerun:d3bdac8`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala), which introduced:
1. **I-Frame Gating on Fast-Attach**: Enforcing GOP keyframe alignment before releasing packets to new attaching clients ([`MpegTsSync.scala:L408-L534`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L408-L534)).
2. **Capped Flap Dampening at 4 seconds**: Preventing runaway exponential retry delays during clustered RF fades ([`ResilientHlsSource.scala:L73`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala#L73)).
3. **Soft-Stall Recovery (4.0s)**: Lowering watchdog latency while maintaining MPEG-TS null keepalive padding.

## 6. Root Cause Technical Deep Dive

### 1. The Roku 33% Buffering Hang: Dead Instance Polling and 68s Socket Timeout
In the Roku SceneGraph `Video` node architecture, playback initialization and buffering progress are rendered in discrete tiers:
- **33%**: Stage 1 — Retrieval, parsing, and initialization of the HLS playlist / DASH manifest.
- **66%**: Stage 2 — Retrieval and demuxing of media segments; audio/video format probing.
- **99%**: Stage 3 — Decryption, pipeline pre-roll buffer fill, and first frame presentation.

When PMS encountered unrecoverable stream corruptions on session `4423f72e` at `20:31:24 EDT`, it logged `Failed to find consumer` and shut down the transcode runner. However, the Roku client's network layer continued requesting the live M3U8 (`buildLiveM3U8: no instance available`). Because the server returned an error on manifest requests, the Roku player could never advance past Stage 1, remaining frozen at **33%** until its internal HTTP socket timeout expired at `20:32:32 EDT` (**exactly 68.4 seconds elapsed**), matching the user's perception of *"feeling like at least a minute before it terminated"*.

### 2. Client-Side State Desynchronization & Stale Session Traps (`Req#78c4`)
When the Roku playback engine failed, the Roku Plex client UI returned to the guide but failed to cleanly nullify its in-memory session reference.
- Upon manual re-tune at `20:32:32 EDT`, the Roku app issued a request referencing the expired request identifier `Req#78c4`.
- PMS rejected the request as orphaned (`Unable to find title for item of type 5` and `Unknown metadata type:`), preventing a new transcode runner from initializing.
- Although `tablo2hdhomerun` received the backend grabber request, canceled idle grace, and aligned the stream on keyframe in 275ms (`00:32:33.122 UTC`), PMS aborted the client session before streaming could begin.

### 3. Architectural Divergence: Direct Stream (`copy`) vs. Full Transcode (`h264`)
This incident highlighted a stark operational difference in client resilience:
- **Direct Stream (Roku Stick 4K)**: The Roku Stick 4K supports MPEG-2 decoding in hardware. Plex opts to copy the raw video stream (`videoDecision="copy"`), repacking raw MPEG-2 TS packets into HLS chunks. This makes the Roku vulnerable to upstream ATSC transmission defects: broken sequence headers, missing I-frames, and macroblock corruptions pass directly into the Roku demuxer, triggering playback aborts.
- **Full Transcode (iPhone / iPad)**: Apple mobile devices do not natively support MPEG-2. Plex routes the stream through its Intel VAAPI GPU transcode engine (`videoDecision="transcode"`), decoding MPEG-2 and re-encoding into compliant H.264 video. FFmpeg's hardware decoder conceals damaged macroblocks (`Warning MVs not available`), maintaining uninterrupted container output. Consequently, when the user picked up the iPhone to validate broadcast health (`20:32:36 EDT`), the channel played smoothly, confirming that the OTA tuner was healthy while the Roku client was locked in an error state.

### 4. Recovery Mechanics of the Cold Roku App Reload
Performing a **cold reload of the Roku app** at `20:33:18 EDT` completely destroyed the tainted SceneGraph `Video` node instance, clearing `Req#78c4` from device memory.
- The fresh app instance initiated a clean tune request (`b78a42cc`).
- Because `tablo2hdhomerun` had already fast-attached and locked the Tablo tuner during the earlier retune probe, [`alignToIFrame`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L408-L534) dropped 4 leading packets in **275ms** and presented a clean keyframe on PID 256.
- The clean keyframe allowed Roku's hardware decoder to initialize its MPEG-2 raster parameters properly, establishing a continuous playback run of **58 minutes 45 seconds** until high-wind macroblock corruption forced a buffer stall at 21:31 EDT.

### 5. Verification of Architectural Milestones (Commit `d3bdac8`)
Despite client-side retune edge cases, the backend proxy delivered unprecedented stability:
- **Single Continuous Hardware Tuner Lease**: Held Tablo lease `1c7188b6` continuously for **1 hour 45 minutes 16 seconds** across 13 retune recoveries without dropping the hardware tuner or forcing a cold ATSC re-scan.
- **Fast-Attach Keyframe Alignment**: [`alignToIFrame`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala#L408-L534) eliminated mid-GOP transcode initialization crashes on fast-attach, aligning in 275ms.
- **Capped Flap Dampening & Soft Recovery**: Capping the dampening window at 4 seconds ensured that **all 13 retunes completed within 6.3 to 7.2 seconds** (average 6.54s), keeping downstream buffers warm via MPEG-TS null packets.
- **Transcode Runner Survival**: PMS transcode runner crashed **0 times** across the entire 105-minute window (`Transcode runner appears to have died` count: 0).

## 7. Architecture & Design Recommendations

### 1. Fix Soft Watchdog Scoping Bug (R1)
- **Action**: In [`Tablo4thGen.scala:1603`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/tuner/Tablo4thGen.scala#L1603), pass `stallTimeout` rather than `softTimeout` as the second argument to `StallWatchdog.flow(softTimeout, stallTimeout, ...)`.
- **Mechanism**: Restricts the 4.0s soft timeout strictly to the pre-first-byte window after a soft recovery. Once data resumes, steady-state stall evaluation reverts to the documented stall timeout.
- **Interval Clamping**: Clamp watchdog check interval in `ResilientHlsSource.scala` to `250.millis` maximum so trip latency is tightly bounded.

### 2. Raise Steady-State Stall Timeout Default to 8s (R2)
- **Action**: Change `STREAM_STALL_TIMEOUT_SEC` default from `5` to `8` in `AppConfig.scala`.
- **Expected Outcome**: Eliminates false-positive watchdog restarts during normal Tablo segment poll jitter (51 gaps clustered at 4.00–4.11s).

### 3. Client Disconnect & Segment Telemetry (R3)
- **Action**:
  - Add structured disconnect logging in `Tablo4thGen.scala` (`outcome`, `durationMs`, `bytesSent`).
  - Add per-segment completion logging in `HlsBackend.scala` with `gapMs`, duration, and sequence.
  - Add `short read` warnings when segment bytes delivered are less than the requested byte-range header.
  - Tie the `real data resumed` log threshold in `ResilientHlsSource.scala` to the configured stall threshold.

### 4. Longer Idle Grace with LRU Eviction (R4)
- **Action**: Increase `SESSION_IDLE_GRACE_SEC` default from `75` to `180`. In `SessionManager`, when tuners are at capacity (`occupied >= tuners`), evict the oldest `IdleGrace` session to service new tune requests rather than rejecting with `503 No available tuners`.

### 5. Connection Hygiene (R5)
- **Action**: Ensure HTTP response entity bytes are safely discarded if downstream cancels before or during segment consumption in `HlsBackend.scala`.

### 6. Client Profile Consideration for Direct Stream Clients
- **Action**: If Direct Stream clients (Roku) experience unrecoverable hardware decoder crashes on fringe channels with heavy macroblock corruption, configure Plex client profiles or playback quality to force server-side VAAPI transcoding to H.264.
