## 2. Executive Summary of Morning Playback

On `2026-10-10`, playback accounting across both `tablo2hdhomerun` and `plex` containers captured four distinct streaming sessions, highlighting both notable resilience achievements from recent commits ([`88067da`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala) and [`b4f37ae`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala)) and remaining failure points during clustered OTA dropouts:

1. **Overnight / Early Morning Session (`03:19:50` – `09:01:46 UTC` / `23:19:50` – `05:01:46 EDT`, 5h 41m 56s)**:
   - Streamed CBS 4.1 (`S20431_004_01`) under lease `c85d1175`.
   - Suffered a single 5-second stall at `05:19:51 UTC` (`01:19:51 EDT`). The watchdog fired and auto-recovered (`attempt=2`) transparently, allowing the session to stream uninterrupted for another 3 hours 40 minutes until client detachment and graceful lease teardown.
2. **Morning Session A (`11:54:03` – `13:29:23 UTC` / `07:54:03` – `09:29:23 EDT`, 95m 20s)**:
   - Streamed CBS 4.1 (`S20431_004_01`) under lease `d0ca3f1f`.
   - Used fast-attach to transfer ownership to client `7c10c0d7` / Plex `b5fe2595` seamlessly.
   - Streamed cleanly for **81 minutes** before encountering back-to-back RF stalls at `13:15 UTC` (`09:15 EDT`). The watchdog executed two successful retunes (`attempt=2` and `attempt=3`), resuming real data in **8.5s** and **8.4s** respectively with clean I-Frame alignment.
   - At `13:27:11 UTC` (`09:27:11 EDT`), another signal drop triggered retune `attempt=4`, resuming in **7.8s**. However, the repeated stutter prompted the user to exit 55 seconds later (`13:28:08 UTC`), entering a 75s idle-grace teardown.
3. **Concurrent Multi-Tuner Session B (`13:18:44 UTC` – Ongoing / `09:18:44 EDT` – Ongoing)**:
   - Streamed PBS 2.3 WGBXDT7 (`S112088_002_03`) under lease `328db06d` with `occupied=2` tuners simultaneously alongside CBS 4.1.
   - Streamed continuously with **zero stalls and zero retunes**, demonstrating that multi-tuner proxy concurrency and Tablo hardware health were operating flawlessly.
4. **Morning Session C (`14:00:26` – `14:06:36 UTC` / `10:00:26` – `10:06:36 EDT`, 6m 10s)**:
   - User re-opened CBS 4.1 on Plex client `B9A0D6A9` under lease `0726ba4c`.
   - Played normally for 104 seconds before an aggressive cluster of ATSC signal dropouts occurred at `14:02:19 UTC` (`10:02:19 EDT`).
   - Although `tablo2hdhomerun` recovered three times in under 8 seconds each (`attempt=2` in 7.4s, `attempt=3` in 7.7s, `attempt=4` in 7.8s) with perfect I-Frame synchronization on PID 256, the rapid succession of dropouts overwhelmed the downstream Plex transcoder.
   - Plex transcoder statistics show playback progress froze permanently at `104188ms` (`10:02:28 EDT`). The player buffered for 125 seconds before switching to `state="paused"` at `10:04:33 EDT`, followed by client disconnect at `10:05:21 EDT`.

### Environmental & Meteorological Correlation
Meteorological records from KMHT highlight clear atmospheric correlation across these sessions:
- **Overnight Calm (11:19 PM – 05:01 AM EDT)**: Surface winds were calm (0–5 mph) under clear skies, yielding near-ideal ATSC 8VSB reception and sustaining 5 hours 41 minutes of continuous playback with only a single transient auto-recovery.
- **Mid-Morning Breeze (07:54 AM – 10:06 AM EDT)**: Post-frost atmospheric warming initiated a northwest surface breeze building to 8–13 mph. Moving tree canopies along the 50-mile Needham-to-NH path introduced transient dynamic multipath fading on UHF RF Channel 20 (CBS 4.1), producing intermittent signal drops. Meanwhile, Physical UHF Channel 32 (PBS 2.3) demonstrated superior link margin, streaming without a single dropout.

## 5. Root Cause Technical Deep Dive

### 5.1 Recovery Latency Cut by 73% (27.5s -> 7.5s)
In yesterday's incident ([`2026.10.09_1.scenario.md`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/docs/playback-failure-scenarios/2026.10.09_1.scenario.md)), the total stream freeze duration was **27.5 seconds**:
- 8.0 seconds for stall detection (`stallTimeoutSec = 8`)
- 19.5 seconds for Tablo hardware tune and playlist materialize

In today's codebase ([`88067da`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala)):
- Stall detection was tightened to **5.0 seconds** (`stallTimeoutSec = 5`).
- The retune handshake returned valid segments within **2.4–2.8 seconds**.
- Total freeze duration dropped to **7.4–7.8 seconds**.

```
Yesterday (2026-10-09):
[---- 8.0s Stall Detection ----][------------- 19.5s Tablo Tune / Playlist -------------] = 27.5s

Today (2026-10-10):
[-- 5.0s --][-- 2.4s --] = 7.4s  (73% reduction in freeze duration)
```

### 5.2 I-Frame Alignment Verification
A key concern in previous scenarios was decoder failure when resuming streams mid-GOP. As demonstrated in today's logs:
```
INFO [stream:hls] emit label=0726ba4c count=1 firstSeq=42 lastSeq=42
INFO Aligned to I-Frame on PID 256 (dropped 0 leading packets)
INFO [4thgen-channel-S20431_004_01] real data resumed after 7407ms
```
The [`MpegTsSync`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/MpegTsSync.scala) stage successfully identified and synchronized to the sequence header / I-Frame boundary on the video PID without packet desynchronization.

### 5.3 Clustered Dropouts vs Downstream Player Tolerances
Despite the 7.4s recovery time, live TV playback failed due to **dropout frequency**. 

When the Tablo lost signal three times in 55 seconds (at `14:02:23`, `14:02:38`, and `14:03:18 UTC`), the player received only 1–2 segments of video between each freeze. This pattern:
1. Starved Plex's DASH chunk transcode buffer.
2. Prevented the client from building a healthy 6–10 second playback buffer.
3. Left the user staring at a frozen frame (`progress="104188"` ms) for over two minutes, resulting in abandonment.

## 6. Architecture & Design Decisions

### Current State
- The 5-second `stallTimeoutSec` and I-Frame alignment significantly reduce recovery latency and eliminate GOP alignment corruption.
- Multi-tuner handling and long-duration playback (evidenced by the 5.7h overnight session and the flawless concurrent PBS 2.3 session) are robust.
- Commit [`18539aa`](file:///home/bdina/git/tablo2hdhomerun/src/main/scala/app/stream/ResilientHlsSource.scala) provides configurable freeze-frame null-packet padding (`STREAM_ENABLE_SLATE_VIDEO=false`), protecting transcoding pipelines from video format mismatches during gaps.

### Architectural Takeaways
1. **RF Specificity**: Channel 4.1's instability alongside Channel 2.3's flawless playback confirms that intermittent dropouts were physical antenna/RF reception anomalies, not system resource constraints or proxy defects.
2. **Back-to-Back Stall Dampening**: When retunes succeed but data stalls again within < 15 seconds, repeated retuning cannot overcome severe physical RF loss. The proxy handled this gracefully by maintaining keepalive null packets and exiting cleanly upon client detachment without crashing or leaving orphaned tuner leases.
